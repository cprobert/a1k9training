## BRONZE OBEDIENCE COURSE

Basic on lead obedience for beginners. Adolescent dogs need the most training and the help of an instructor and the support of other people training their dogs will be invaluable to you.

Rescue and rehomed dogs are just as welcome, and can start whenever you're ready — some owners like a couple of weeks together at home first to settle in, but there's no need to wait. And if you're here because your dog pulls on the lead or won't come back when you call, you're far from alone — it's one of the most common reasons owners get in touch, and exactly what this course puts right.
