## JUNIOR COURSE

The junior course is especially developed for young puppies between the ages of 6 to 12 months and is suitable for all breeds of dogs. It is a natural progression from puppy classes.

Rescue and rehomed dogs are just as welcome here, and can start whenever you're ready. And if you've come because your dog pulls on the lead or won't come back when called, that's exactly what this course is for — it's what most owners get in touch about.
